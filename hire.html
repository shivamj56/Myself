What I build for clients

AI products and LLM features

Résumé and document analysis, assistants that answer from your own data, and features that turn model output into structured data your app can trust. I put Groq, Google Gemini, OpenAI or Claude behind one client with fallbacks, and I make the app show a clear error instead of a made-up answer. Example: how I built NexeraAI.

Full-stack web apps

Next.js, React and TypeScript on the front; Node.js, PostgreSQL with Prisma, sign-in, queues and scheduled jobs on the back. Example: NexeraAI's job importer, which pulls real listings from public job boards and removes duplicates.

Chrome extensions and developer tools

Extensions that read the page you're on and do real work with it. Example: Clonix measures a live website's colours, type and spacing, and writes them where Cursor, Claude Code and Windsurf look (how it reads a page).

Android, Flutter and Android TV apps

Example: Orbit OS, an open-source app that sets up a Google TV from a phone with a pure-Dart ADB client, plus a TV launcher that runs at 60 fps on a low-memory TV (how I built it).

Websites that Google and AI assistants understand

Fast pages with structured data, sitemaps and llms.txt, so search engines and AI answers describe your product correctly. This site and the Orbit OS guides are built that way.

Products I've built and launched

  • NexeraAI: an AI career platform. It scores your résumé, matches you to real open jobs and shows the skills you're missing. Live.
