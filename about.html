Who is Shivam Anil Jha?

Shivam Anil Jha (also written Shivam Jha) is an AI architect and full-stack developer from Mumbai, India. He is a co-founder of Sqaron Technologies, a product studio, and builds AI products and apps end to end: the idea, the design, the front end and the AI behind it.

What has he built?

  • NexeraAI: an AI career platform with résumé analysis, referrals, tech jobs and personalised learning roadmaps (how I built it).
  • Clonix: turns any website into design tokens, components and packs for Cursor, Claude Code and Windsurf, or clones it into a runnable ZIP (how it reads a website).
  • Orbit OS: an open-source (GPL-3.0) app that sets up Google TV and Android TV from a phone, free on Google Play (code on GitHub; how I built it).
  • Website concepts for a corporate cab and tours company: Obsidian, Golden Hour and Kinetic.

What is Sqaron Technologies?

Sqaron Technologies is the product studio Shivam Anil Jha co-founded in India. It builds websites, mobile apps, SaaS products, AI agents, Chrome extensions and backends, one team from the first call to launch, with a written plan and a fixed price within 72 hours.
